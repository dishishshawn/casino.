## 1. Project History and Why This PRD Exists

Casino v1 (PRD `prd_v1_edllls.txt`, 2026-05-03) targeted Earnings-Drift LLM Long-Short on US large-caps: classical PEAD signal augmented with Claude-scored earnings-call transcripts. After 8 weeks of disciplined empirical work, **v1 is permanently dead**:

| Test | Sharpe | Q4-Q0 spread | Verdict |
|------|--------|--------------|---------|
| Pure SUE PEAD (S&P 500, 40 quarters of EPS history, 20d hold) | -0.42 | +0.44% / 10d | FAIL |
| Composite SUE+SURGE+\|SUE\|·sign(60d_mom) | -0.36 | +0.14% / 10d | FAIL |
| **FinBERT text-PEAD (3000 transcripts, 2018-vintage frozen LLM, no look-ahead bias)** | **-0.60** | **-1.05% / 20d gross** | **KILL** |

The text-PEAD result is decisive: positive-sentiment earnings calls *underperform* negative-sentiment ones by -1.05% over 20 days, gross of cost. Sign is wrong, not just magnitude. This matches the published literature (Martineau 2022 *CFR*, Subrahmanyam 2025, Hirshleifer-Peng-Wang 2025 *RFS*, Lan et al. 2025 *FRL*) — large-cap PEAD has been arbitraged away post-2006; surviving alpha is concentrated in microcap names a $25-50k retail account cannot trade.

A 2026-05-05 deep-research audit recommended:
- **Do NOT subscribe to FMP Ultimate at $1788/yr** (violates the project's own 2% NAV data-cost rule by 2-4× at $25-50k NAV; expected net Sharpe -0.7 to +0.3 even with paid transcripts).
- **Pivot to Branch C** — free-signal stack on retail-tradeable cross-asset ETFs and SEC EDGAR data.

Casino v2 implements Branch C. This PRD describes what to build, in what order, and the invariants that must be preserved.

## 2. Strategy: Branch C Free-Signal Stack

### 2.1 Core thesis

Diversified time-series momentum on liquid cross-asset ETFs is the highest-Sharpe documented retail-realistic factor that does not depend on microcap exposure (Hurst-Ooi-Pedersen 2017, Moskowitz-Ooi-Pedersen 2012 *JFE*). Combining it with one or two uncorrelated complementary signals exploits the diversification benefit (~sqrt(N) of independent signals) to lift portfolio Sharpe meaningfully above any single signal.

### 2.2 Universe

10 cross-asset ETFs, retail-tradeable on Alpaca cash account, no margin:

- **US equity:** SPY, QQQ, IWM
- **International equity:** EFA, EEM
- **Treasuries:** TLT (long), IEF (intermediate)
- **Commodities:** GLD (gold), DBC (broad), USO (oil)

### 2.3 Signal stack

**Step 1 — TS-Momentum (TSMOM) — STATUS: COMPLETE.**

`casino/signals/ts_momentum.py` + `casino/backtest/tsmom_baseline.py`.

- Equal-weighted sign blend of 1m / 3m / 6m / 12m total returns.
- Per-asset volatility target 10% annualized via 60d realized-vol scaling, capped at 3× leverage.
- Portfolio gross capped at 1.0 (Hurst-Ooi-Pedersen equal-risk normalization).
- Long-only mode (clip negative weights to zero) — long_short underperforms net of cost.
- Monthly rebalance.

**Empirical result (strict-OOS 2006-2026, 12 bps round-trip, 5051 obs):**

| Metric | Value | Gate | Result |
|--------|-------|------|--------|
| Sharpe | 0.704 | ≥ 0.55 | PASS |
| Deflated SR (n_trials=10) | +0.97 | > 0 | PASS (p=0.166) |
| Deflated SR (n_trials=20) | +0.67 | > 0 | PASS (p=0.252) |
| Deflated SR (n_trials=30) | +0.51 | > 0 | PASS (p=0.306) |
| Max Drawdown | -19.5% | > -25% | PASS |
| Annualized Return | 7.23% | — | PASS |

Deflated SR is positive at all honest n_trials but does not clear p<0.05 at any n_trials with 20-year sample. That is the borderline-but-real zone. Per researcher: paper-trade-ready, not live-cash-ready.

**Step 2 — Cross-Asset Carry — STATUS: TODO (highest priority).**

Koijen-Moskowitz-Pedersen-Vrugt (2018, *JFE*) cross-asset carry on the same 10-ETF universe. Strictly dominates Form 4 on every dimension (same data, lower correlation to TSMOM, higher Sharpe).

- **Bond carry:** trailing 10y yield − front-month T-bill yield (FRED data, free).
- **Equity carry:** ETF distribution yield − T-bill yield.
- **Commodity carry:** DBC front-vs-back month roll proxy from term-structure (free via futures.io if needed; can defer if too complex).
- Rank assets cross-sectionally by carry; long high-carry / short low-carry (or long-only top half).

Gate: standalone Sharpe ≥ 0.4, deflated SR > 0 at n_trials=30, **realized correlation with TSMOM < 0.4 on OOS sample**. If correlation > 0.4, carry is not adding what KMPV claims and needs diagnosis before integration.

Estimated retail-realistic standalone Sharpe: 0.40-0.65 net of 12 bps RT.

**Step 3 — Optional third leg (Form 4 cluster purchases or short-term mean-reversion).**

Only after Step 2 clears its gate. Form 4 expected Sharpe 0.20-0.40 (Aktas et al. 2024, Allredge-Blank 2017 cluster premium decayed); short-term reversal expected 0.30-0.50 with negative correlation to TSMOM. Decide which based on what's most additive to the TSMOM+Carry ensemble.

### 2.4 Live-cash gate (preserved from v1 PRD §6.3 + 11.5)

No live trading until ALL of the following:

1. **3+ months of paper trading history** on the live ensemble.
2. **Deflated Sharpe of the ensemble > 0 at n_trials=30** (Bailey-Lopez de Prado 2014).
3. **Realized live paper P&L is statistically indistinguishable from backtest** at 95% confidence via Kolmogorov-Smirnov test.
4. **Either Form 4 / 3rd leg has cleared its own DSR gate, OR explicit acceptance of 2-signal ensemble.**
5. **Backtrader event-driven validator** has confirmed the vbt result within 10% Sharpe degradation (catches signal/execution alignment bugs).
6. **Kill switch** flattens all positions in under 60 seconds, tested end-to-end.

### Hard restart rule
If at any point a previously-PASSED gate FAILs on more rigorous re-test (e.g., post-2006 strict-OOS Sharpe drops below 0.5 after vol-target sensitivity grid), treat the prior result as a discovery artifact and restart Branch C at zero. **The 8 weeks already spent on EDLLLS are sunk cost; the next 90 days should be spent generating an additional, diversified, well-validated track record — not another single-signal backtest.**

#### AMENDMENT 2026-05-07 — Branch C 30-day cap (overrides PRD §6.3 / §2.4 for TSMOM-alone paper)

**Context.** Task 37 (sensitivity grid) FAIL'd at the 56th percentile and DSR n_trials=90 = -1.40. Task 38 (carry signal) FAIL'd standalone at Sharpe -0.11, DSR -3.13; ensemble gain over TSMOM ≈ 0. Per the §3 hard-restart rule the prior TSMOM gate is a discovery artifact.

**User decision (verbatim).** "Don't add a leg. Paper-trade TSMOM alone, 30-day cap on Branch C, then commit or kill." The user explicitly accepts the risk of paper-trading the failed-sensitivity TSMOM config in exchange for a fixed deadline that prevents indefinite signal-hunting.

**Scope override.** The original 3-month paper window (PRD §6.3 / §2.4) is reduced to a binding **30-day cap** for Branch C TSMOM-alone. The 90-day plan §3 weeks 9-12 schedule is suspended for Branch C; ensemble work (tasks 39-40) and Form 4 (task 42) remain BLOCKED. After day 30 a binary COMMIT-or-KILL verdict is emitted by `casino.execution.tsmom_clock_check --verdict`.

**The 30-day cap regime is operationally encoded in two modules:**
- `casino/execution/tsmom_runner.py` — monthly rebal (manual run only; the runner does NOT cron-schedule itself; the 30-day clock starts when the operator first invokes it on a month-end).
- `casino/execution/tsmom_clock_check.py` — daily kill-criteria monitor + day-30 verdict CLI.

**Long-only deviation.** Alpaca paper *cash* accounts cannot short. The runner forces `mode="long_only"` regardless of caller intent. The variant being validated on paper is therefore TSMOM-long-only, not the long-short variant in the backtest. This is intentional: documenting the deviation and accepting that paper P&L is not a 1:1 read on the long-short backtest.

**Kill criteria (any one fires → flatten + disable + Discord alert + persisted `kill_event` row):**
1. **Drawdown** > 10% of starting NAV (vbt baseline MaxDD -29% over 20yr → 10% in 30 days is >2σ tail; suspect signal/exec misalignment).
2. **Single-day** P&L drop > 5% NAV (sizing or risk-cap bug).
3. **Cap violation** — gross > 1.0+0.02 buffer or any single-name > 10% NAV (CLAUDE.md hard-rule caps).
4. **Reconcile drift** > 1% NAV (broker-vs-book; fill-tracking bug we cannot afford to discover live).
5. **KS-test divergence** at day ≥ 14, paper daily returns vs `reports/tsmom_backtest_returns.csv`, two-sample p < 0.01 → kill.

**Day-30 COMMIT preconditions (ALL required):**
1. Zero kill_event rows.
2. ≥ 1 monthly rebalance completed end-to-end.
3. Reconcile drift now < 0.5% NAV.
4. Paper Sharpe ≥ -0.2 (deliberately loose; 30 days is a tiny sample).
5. KS-test p > 0.10 (or unavailable — treated as PASS with caveat).

Otherwise: KILL.

**COMMIT does NOT authorize live trading.** It means "Branch C lives; the operator now decides whether to (a) start ensemble work with a different complementary signal, (b) extend paper trading toward the live-cash gate (PRD §2.4), or (c) park Branch C and start a new branch". KILL means abandon TSMOM-alone, reassess. The hard-restart rule in §3 still applies.

**The full PRD §2.4 live-cash gate (3+ months paper, ensemble DSR > 0 at n_trials=30, KS distribution match, Backtrader validator, kill-switch end-to-end) remains in force.** The 30-day cap is a *Branch C survival check*, not a substitute for the live-cash gate.

**Operational guarantees encoded in code (see also `tests/test_tsmom_runner.py`, `tests/test_tsmom_clock_check.py`, `tests/test_kill_switch.py`):**
- Runner refuses to run if `ALPACA_BASE_URL` does not contain `paper` (defense in depth against env-var typo flipping live).
- Every entry order is a bracket order with broker-side stop at -10% (CLAUDE.md hard rule 3).
- All caps in `casino/execution/risk.py` are enforced; the runner does not add a parallel sizing path.
- Kill-switch end-to-end re-verified post-task-36 in `tests/test_kill_switch.py`.

## 5. Key invariants (preserved from v1, with one update)

1. **`data/store.py` is the only path to DuckDB/Parquet.** Other modules import helpers; they don't open DuckDB directly.
2. **Money is `Decimal`** end-to-end through orders, fills, the SQLite book. Floats acceptable for research-only score computation in `signals/`.
3. **All times stored UTC.** Convert to America/New_York only at display boundaries.
4. **`execution/risk.py` is authoritative for sizing, stops, kill switch.** Brokers and signals are inputs to risk; risk is not advisory.
5. **`execution/reconcile.py` is the source of truth for "what we actually hold."** Read from reconcile, not from broker API.
6. **No live cash without:** 3+ months paper history, ensemble Deflated Sharpe > 0 at n_trials=30, Backtrader event-driven validator confirmation, KS-test distribution match.
7. **Per-trade risk ≤ 1.5% NAV; max single-name 10%; gross exposure ≤ 100%.** Sizing helpers in `risk.py` enforce these; do not bypass.
8. **Fractional Kelly only (¼ to ½).** Full-Kelly forbidden.
9. **Cash account, not margin,** until 6+ months profitable live history.
10. **Cost ceiling:** monthly data + API spend ≤ 2% of expected monthly P&L. **Hard rule violated by FMP Ultimate at <$150k NAV; do not subscribe.**
11. **(NEW v2)** **No yfinance index-proxy backfill in production backtests.** ETF inception date is the strict-OOS start. Pre-inception data is for sanity-check only, never the headline number.

## 6. Success / failure criteria (revised for v2)

Project successful by Day 90 (i.e., 2026-08-05) if all of:

1. The TSMOM + carry ensemble pipeline runs unattended on local machine or $5/mo VPS.
2. Ensemble Sharpe ≥ 0.5 with deflated SR > 0 at n_trials=30 on strict-OOS 2006-2026.
3. 3+ months of paper trading on the ensemble, with realized P&L distribution KS-test-indistinguishable from backtest at 95%.
4. Monthly all-in operating cost ≤ $50.
5. Kill switch tested end-to-end; flattens positions in under 60 seconds.

Project paused (not "iterated on") if any of:

- Ensemble deflated SR ≤ 0 after honest evaluation.
- Live paper Sharpe diverges from backtest Sharpe by > 50%.
- Drawdown exceeds 25% in paper trading.
- Carry signal does not meet its standalone gate AND no other signal can be found that does.
- Strict-OOS TSMOM re-tested at any point and Sharpe drops below 0.5.

## 7. Out of scope for v2

- Pure US large-cap stock-picking (PEAD, momentum cross-section, etc.). Killed empirically.
- LLM-scored signals on transcripts, news, 8-Ks. Look-ahead bias unfixable without fixed-vintage models; even FinBERT text-PEAD reversed sign on the kill-test.
- Microcap exposure. Outside retail liquidity capacity at $25-50k account.
- Options market making, HFT infrastructure, anything requiring institutional-grade data feeds.
- 13F holdings change signal. 45-day reporting lag makes it not actionable for retail at the strategy frequency Casino runs.
- Crypto, FX, options-implied vol surface anomalies. Out of declared US-only scope; high operational complexity.
